Light

One soft white key from top-left. Highlights are white at 50% on the upper-left of every coin; blooms sit in opposite corners of the field.

Value, not hue

Hierarchy is done entirely with brightness: #F2F2F2 for what matters, #A1A1A1 for the rest, #666 for labels. Even "down" is just a darker grey.

No tint

Renders drift blue or warm easily. Check a mid-grey pixel: R, G and B must be within 3 of each other or it is off-brand.

04 Materials & motifs · the site's own CSS renders (key-visuals/src/kv.css) — use as image references

materials & motifs

Dark glass, brushed silver, white light.

Dark glass tiles (quilted, rounded squares, 84px pitch, faint white edge), brushed-silver coins (thick discs, satin face, bright rim), optical glass (the prism). One soft white key light, black falloff.

Quilted glass field — the groundRounded-square relief in near-black, white edge at 16%, faint white blooms in opposite corners. Every hero stands on this.
Silver coins — the tokenThick satin-silver discs floating above the field, bright brushed rim, soft black ground shadow. One coin = one basket.
The prism — the ideaOptical glass on black. A thin white beam enters, a soft fan of light leaves. This is the logo and the hero object at once.

Light & camera recipe

Key: one soft white light, top-left, like a large softbox. Highlights read as satin, not mirror.
Fill: none. Shadows go to true black. No ambient colour.
Camera: three-quarter top-down (~35°) for coins on the field; straight-on for the prism and the phone.
Depth: mostly sharp; slight falloff on far coins. The prism's fan of light is soft by nature.
Framing: coins in the corners, copy owns the centre-left. Half the frame is empty dark field.
Finish: satin metal, clean glass. No grain, no chromatic fringing, no lens flare.

Motion — video prompts

Slow, weightless motion. Coins drift up and down a few pixels on a 9 second cycle, alternating, never spinning fast. Camera locked off or a very slow push-in. Light stays constant. Reveal by a white beam entering the prism and fanning out. End on a held frame with the centre empty and black.

06 Do / Don't · the checks that keep a render on-brand

do / don't

If it has a colour, it is off-brand.

75% black · 20% graphite · 5% silver and white
one soft white light · shadows go to black

Do

Pure black ground. #000000, not charcoal, not navy. Surfaces are graphite on top of it.
Strict greyscale. Every pixel has R = G = B. Hierarchy comes from brightness alone.
Brushed satin silver, not chrome. A soft highlight, a bright rim, no mirror reflections.
Thick coins and quilted tiles are the only objects; the prism is the only glass.
One soft white key light from top-left. Shadows fall to true black.
Objects in the corners, copy in the centre-left. Half the frame stays empty.
Calm motion. Things drift and settle; nothing spins, flies or explodes.

Don't

No colour, ever. Not green, not blue, not gold. The most common drift is a cool blue tint on the silver.
No rainbow through the prism. White in, white fan out. It is not the Pink Floyd cover.
No neon, glow, bloom or lens flare. Light is soft and photographic.
No chrome mirror finish and no gold. Silver is satin and brushed.
No crypto clichés: coins with faces, candlesticks, rockets, bulls, bears, vault doors.
No people, hands or mascots. The product is the object.
No baked-in text. Generate clean plates; type is set afterwards in Schibsted Grotesk.
