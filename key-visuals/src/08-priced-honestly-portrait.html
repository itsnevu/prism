prism

Priced honestly,
even when half the market is asleep.

Equities close. Silver closes. Oil closes. Feeds go quiet over the weekend. Prism prices each leg from an oracle and refuses to quote a basket while any leg is stale.

  • Oracle-priced
    Every leg is marked from a live feed — tokenized equities, silver and oil alike — so NAV is a number, not a guess.
  • Paused, not guessed
    When a leg goes stale, mint and redeem pause for that basket instead of inventing a price.
  • Capped per asset
    No single name can swallow a basket. Weights are pulled back inside a band when they drift.
